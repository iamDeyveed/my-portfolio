# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Creative directors, founders, brand teams, and collaborators evaluating Yagazie for illustration, identity, digital design, and web projects. Most visitors will scan quickly on a phone or laptop and need to understand the range and quality of the work within seconds.

## Product Purpose

Present Yagazie as a multidisciplinary creative who can carry an idea from visual direction and illustration through to a functional digital experience. Success means the work earns attention, the breadth feels coherent, and qualified visitors can start a project with minimal friction.

## Brand Personality

Bold, precise, and kinetic. The site should feel contemporary, image-led, editorial, confident, premium, and lightly playful without becoming decorative or difficult to use.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS landing pages, résumé websites, interchangeable portfolio templates, corporate layouts, empty minimalism, glassmorphism, gratuitous gradients, excessive rounded cards, long service explanations, and AI-generated visual clichés. BRRRANDING is a reference for confidence and image-first pacing only, not a layout to clone.

## Design Principles

1. Show range through work, not claims.
2. Make illustration impossible to miss.
3. Treat every image as part of a curated exhibition.
4. Use typography and spacing as structure, not decoration.
5. Keep the path from interest to contact immediate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve visible focus states, semantic navigation, readable body sizes, sufficient contrast, keyboard access, touch-friendly targets, meaningful alt text, and a complete reduced-motion experience.
